# AI Prompts

## Personalized Audit Summary Prompt

```text
You are an AI financial assistant helping startups optimize AI tool spending.

Analyze the user's AI stack and generate a concise 100-word professional summary.

Focus on:
- overspending areas
- optimization opportunities
- estimated savings
- practical recommendations

Keep the tone professional, concise, and actionable.
```

---

## Why This Prompt

The prompt is designed to:
- keep summaries concise
- avoid hallucinations
- prioritize actionable insights
- maintain professional SaaS tone

---

## Fallback Strategy

If the AI API fails:
- generate rule-based template summary
- still provide savings insights
- avoid blocking user experience
