
# User Interviews & Research

AI Spend Audit Platform

---

# Objective

The purpose of these interviews is to understand:

* how users currently manage AI software spending
* common pain points
* tool usage behavior
* optimization opportunities
* willingness to pay for AI cost analysis

---

# Interview Questions

## General Questions

1. Which AI tools do you currently use?

2. How much do you spend monthly on AI software?

3. Do you use multiple AI tools simultaneously?

4. How do you currently track AI software spending?

5. Have you ever felt you were overpaying for AI tools?

---

## Optimization Questions

6. Have you switched AI plans before to reduce costs?

7. What factors influence your AI tool choices?

8. Would you trust AI-generated optimization recommendations?

9. How valuable would instant savings analysis be for your team?

10. What would make you pay for this platform?

---

# Key Findings

## Finding 1 — Users Overlap AI Tools

Many users subscribe to:

* ChatGPT
* Claude
* Cursor
* Copilot

at the same time.

This creates:

* duplicated functionality
* unnecessary spending

---

## Finding 2 — Lack of Visibility

Most users:

* do not calculate yearly AI spending
* cannot compare plans easily
* do not optimize subscriptions regularly

---

## Finding 3 — Teams Scale Costs Quickly

Small teams often underestimate how quickly:

```txt
team size × subscription cost
```

increases total spending.

---

## Finding 4 — Users Want Simplicity

Users prefer:

* instant recommendations
* simple dashboards
* clear savings estimates

instead of complex analytics platforms.
