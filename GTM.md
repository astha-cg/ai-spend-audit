# Go-To-Market Strategy

AI Spend Audit Platform

---

# Vision

Help startups, developers, and companies reduce unnecessary AI software spending through intelligent cost optimization and AI-powered recommendations.

---

# Problem

Companies are rapidly adopting AI tools like:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Cursor
- GitHub Copilot
- Gemini
- Windsurf

Most teams:
- overpay for unused plans
- duplicate tools
- choose incorrect pricing tiers
- lack visibility into AI spending

There is currently no simple platform focused on AI SaaS cost optimization.

---

# Solution

AI Spend Audit provides:

- Instant AI cost analysis
- Personalized optimization recommendations
- Monthly + annual savings estimation
- AI-generated audit summaries
- Downloadable PDF reports
- Email-based audit delivery
- Team-based optimization insights
