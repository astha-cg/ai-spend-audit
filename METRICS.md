# Product Metrics

AI Spend Audit Platform

---

# Purpose

This document defines the key product, business, and technical metrics used to measure the growth and performance of the AI Spend Audit platform.

---

# Core Product Metrics

## 1. Total Audits Generated

Tracks the total number of completed AI spend audits.

Purpose:

* measure platform usage
* validate user demand
* monitor growth

Formula:

```txt id="m1"
Total Audits = count(all completed audits)
```

---

# 2. Monthly Active Users (MAU)

Tracks unique users interacting with the platform monthly.

Purpose:

* monitor engagement
* evaluate retention
* measure adoption

Formula:

```txt id="m2"
MAU = unique users per month
```

---

# 3. Daily Active Users (DAU)

Tracks daily user activity.

Purpose:

* measure product stickiness
* monitor short-term engagement

Formula:

```txt id="m3"
DAU = unique users per day
```

---

# 4. Audit Completion Rate

Percentage of users who finish the audit after starting it.

Purpose:

* identify UX friction
* improve onboarding flow

Formula:

```txt id="m4"
Completion Rate =
completed audits / started audits × 100
```

---

# Savings Metrics

## 5. Average Monthly Savings

Tracks the average monthly savings identified by the platform.

Purpose:

* demonstrate product value
* improve marketing credibility

Formula:

```txt id="m5"
Average Savings =
total estimated savings / total audits
```

---

# 6. Total Savings Identified

Measures total potential savings discovered across all users.

Purpose:

* social proof
* investor metric
* business impact tracking

Formula:

```txt id="m6"
Total Savings =
sum(all estimated savings)
```

---

# 7. Annual Savings Projection

Converts monthly savings into yearly value.

Purpose:

* improve perceived ROI
* support enterprise sales

Formula:

```txt id="m7"
Annual Savings =
monthly savings × 12
```

---

# Recommendation Metrics

# 9. Most Common Downgrades

Tracks plans users switch away from most frequently.

Purpose:

* identify overspending patterns
* improve intelligence system

Examples:

* Enterprise → Team
* Team → Pro

# 10. Recommendation Acceptance Rate

Percentage of users who follow platform recommendations.

Purpose:

* evaluate recommendation quality
* measure trust

Formula:

```txt id="m8"
Acceptance Rate =
accepted recommendations / total recommendations
```

Measures speed of:

* audit generation

# 14. Error Rate

Tracks failed requests or broken functionality.

Purpose:

* reliability monitoring
* bug tracking

Formula:

```txt id="m9"

```

# Revenue Metrics

## 18. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

Tracks predictable subscription revenue.

Formula:

```txt id="m10"
MRR =
paying users × subscription price
```

---

# 19. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Measures cost to acquire a user.

Formula:

```txt id="m11"
CAC =
marketing spend / new customers
```

---

# 20. Lifetime Value (LTV)

Measures total expected revenue per user.

Formula:

```txt id="m12"
LTV =
average revenue × customer lifespan
```
